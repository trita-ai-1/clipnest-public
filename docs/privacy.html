ClipNest privacy policy

Effective date: 29 September 2026

tritalabs provides ClipNest. For privacy questions, contact contact@tritalabs.com.

Information on your phone

ClipNest stores video URLs, titles, file locations, download status and preferences on your device. Clipboard content is read only when you tap Paste link. Shared links are received when you choose ClipNest in Android's share sheet. No ClipNest account, social password or login cookie is required.

Network connections

Your phone sends the requested video URL and connection information, including its IP address, directly to the video platform and its media servers. These providers process requests under their own policies. ClipNest does not operate a download backend. If you enable encrypted DNS in Settings, domain queries and connection information are sent to Cloudflare's DNS service. The option is off by default.

Advertising and analytics

This distribution uses Google AdMob. Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform support advertising and privacy choices. Google may process IP addresses (including to estimate general location), device and advertising identifiers, app launches, taps, ad interactions, crash logs and performance diagnostics for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and related purposes. Consent choices are requested where required and available choices can be reopened in Settings. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Provider retention practices are governed by those policies.

Learn more in Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps. Where available, reopen advertising privacy choices in ClipNest Settings. Choosing not to consent does not guarantee that all advertising data processing stops; the available ad types and lawful processing depend on your region and choices.

Permissions and file sharing

Internet access retrieves media. A foreground service and wake lock support user-started background downloads. Notifications are optional. ClipNest manages its own downloaded files through Android MediaStore without requesting access to all files. If you choose Share, the selected file is provided to the app or recipient you select.

Retention and deletion

History and preferences stay on your device until you remove entries or clear app storage. Delete video removes the file and its history entry. Temporary files are removed after completion, failure or cancellation; interrupted attempts are cleaned on the next launch. Uninstalling removes private app data, but completed files in Downloads may remain and can be removed with your file manager. No ClipNest user account exists to delete.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when data practices change and revise the effective date. You can access the policy from Settings. Contact contact@tritalabs.com with questions.
